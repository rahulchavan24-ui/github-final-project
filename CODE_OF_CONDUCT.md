
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may take appropriate action based on the severity of the behavior, including a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from the project.
